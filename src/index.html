    How the correction EQ works: it corrects your bounce, goes in its own Channel EQ, and is updated, never stacked
    1. It corrects your bounce. The app measures the bounce as it is, including any EQ already on your Stereo Out, and suggests a correction on top of it. Your own EQ stays as it is.
    2. It goes in its own Channel EQ. In Logic, add one Channel EQ named "Mixdown correction" on the Stereo Out, after your own master EQ and before any compressor, limiter or Mastering Assistant. Never load the preset into your own EQ: a preset replaces all of a plugin's settings.
    3. It's updated, never stacked. Bounce again with the correction in place and reload: the app suggests the updated whole correction. Load it into the same "Mixdown correction" EQ, replacing its settings, rather than adding another EQ.

        Measure Logic's Channel EQ so presets sound in Logic the way they do here

        1. Download the test noise and the calibration preset, and put the preset in the Channel EQ presets folder (see "Installing presets" in Help).
        2. In Logic, make a new stereo audio track, drag the noise to the start of bar 1, and keep its fader at 0 dB and pan centred. Insert Channel EQ as its only plugin and choose Mixdown Report › Mixdown Calibration. Mute other tracks and leave the Stereo Out empty.
        3. Bounce the 30 seconds of noise: File › Bounce › Project or Section, PCM, WAVE, 24-bit, Normalize off.
        4. Drop the bounce below. The app compares it with the original noise, which gives Channel EQ's exact response, and shows how it differs from the app's model.
        5. Use the calibration. From then on, every exported preset (correction and track EQs) is converted so your Logic's Channel EQ plays exactly the curve the app suggested and tested.

        Bouncing from Logic Pro

        • File > Bounce > Project or Section (⌘B), with the cycle area covering the whole song.
        • Destination PCM, File Format WAVE or AIFF, Resolution 24-bit, Sample Rate same as the project.
        • Normalize: Off. Dithering: None. Mode: Offline.
        • For a mix headed to mastering, bypass any limiter or Mastering Assistant on the Stereo Out first.
        • Make a reference comparable: use a lossless or high-quality file, not a YouTube rip.

        Installing presets in Logic Pro
        1. In Finder, press ⇧⌘G and go to
        2. Put the downloaded .pst files there. The .zip contains a Mixdown Report folder: copy the whole folder so the presets appear together in a submenu.
        3. Track presets: open a Channel EQ on the channel strip and pick the preset from the Setting menu at the top of the plug-in window. Use an empty Channel EQ if the track already has one you want to keep.
        4. The correction preset: load it into a separate Channel EQ named "Mixdown correction" on the Stereo Out, after your own master EQ and before any compressor or limiter. When the app suggests an updated correction, load it into that same EQ.

        Apple doesn't document the preset format; it was mapped from Logic's own presets. Mid/Side processing isn't included yet. The Calibrate tab checks every band on your Logic version and corrects exports if Logic's filters behave differently.
